Why We Ask for Item Protection Fee

We do this to keep our rental service fair and secure for everyone. We require a fully refundable deposit on each item. This deposit acts as a safeguard—protecting the quality of our items and ensuring they’re returned in good condition.

You’ll get your entire deposit back as long as the item is returned on time and without damage. It’s not a fee. It’s a temporary hold that helps us maintain high standards and offer reliable rentals to all our customers.

We believe in transparency, so here’s what you can expect:

  • ✅ 100% refund if the item is returned safely
  • 🔹 Quick inspection upon return
  • 🔸 Clear communication if any issues arise

This system helps us keep prices low, items in great shape, and trust high between renters and owners.
